The facility:

The products are manufactured at a GMP-compliant facility in Cincinnati, Ohio. The facility maintains documented Standard Operating Procedures for every stage of production, from raw material receipt through batch release.

The Cincinnati location handles formulation, blending, filling, packaging, and quality assurance for all formulated products. 

Traceability:

Every product carries a lot code that links the finished product back through the production batch record, the ingredient lot numbers, the suppliers of those ingredients, and in most cases, the farm or harvest origin.

A single lot code allows full traceability in both directions. A finished product traces back to the ingredients that built it. An ingredient lot traces forward to every product it went into. The system has been in place since 2011 and remains part of the documented quality control process used throughout manufacturing, filling, and finished batch release.

Ingredient sourcing:

Suppliers are vetted on three criteria: traceability, growing practice, and analytical purity. Each criterion is documented before approval, and each supplier provides Certificates of Analysis with every shipment, matched against the written specification for that material.

A specification is written before a material is sourced, not after. Approval is conditional and reviewed at set intervals. A supplier that drifts from specification is removed from the qualified list. Where the origin warrants it, the work extends to farm-level visits and harvest verification.

 
WHAT IS NOT IN THE PRODUCTS:

The following are excluded from formulations:

Synthetic vitamin isolates, including folic acid, cyanocobalamin, and pyridoxine hydrochloride. Where vitamins appear in formulas, they come from whole-plant or fermented whole-food sources in their naturally occurring forms. 

No Artificial colors, flavors, sweeteners, or preservatives. 

No Pesticides, chemical solvent residues, GMOs, or irradiated ingredients. 

No Animal products in vegan-labeled formulations.

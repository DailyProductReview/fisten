The questions people actually ask before they click.

Do I have to buy anything to join?

No. Joining is completely free — no cost, no obligation. You can start referring customers and earning 30% retail commission without any purchase requirement. Purchasing PV is what unlocks the deeper matrix and team streams, but it is optional.

How fast do I actually get paid?

In real time. Commissions are credited as orders are processed — not at the end of a month, and never "pending" for weeks.

What happens if my upline goes inactive?

Compression kicks in. Your earnings pass up to the next active participant in the structure — you're never blocked by someone else's inactivity.

Why does starting early matter so much?

Because the matrix is 15 levels deep, and every person who joins under you stays in your structure permanently. The earlier you position, the more of that depth you sit above — and the longer it pays. This is a structural fact of how matrices work, not a marketing line.

Do I need a big team to earn?

No. Retail commission is 30% from day one, without a team. The matrix and binary streams exist for people who choose to build, but you don't need a downline to earn on your own customers.

Is this a get-rich-quick scheme?

No. Zilara pays on real product movement and requires consistent activity. Results depend entirely on your effort and the effort of your team. Anyone promising guaranteed income is lying to you.

What if I join and decide it's not for me?

Joining is free, so there's no financial risk in looking. You can explore the plan, the products, and the back office before deciding how much you want to build.
